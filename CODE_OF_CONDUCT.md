# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration space.

Be constructive, assume good intent while responding to observable behavior, respect privacy boundaries, and make room for people with different experience levels and backgrounds. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publication of private evidence, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens people, privacy, project integrity, or the community. Reports should use the private process described in `SECURITY.md`; do not post sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid disclosing reporter information beyond those who need it to respond.
